Independent fact-checking

TruthBased.org tests contested claims against primary sources: government data, peer-reviewed research, court records, and official statistics. Each article states what the evidence supports, presents the strongest opposing case, and says what would change the conclusion.

Political Fact Checks

Claims politicians make, measured against the record

  • Voter FraudMeasures the 1,620 proven cases in the Heritage Foundation database against 2.5 billion ballots, breaks fraud down by type, reviews the outcome of every 2020 lawsuit and the Fulton County FBI raid, and identifies the one confirmed election law violation by a major political operation in Georgia's 2024 cycle.
  • Border Crossings25 years of CBP encounter data, with the Title 42 recidivism inflation quantified and the methodology changes that break most year-to-year comparisons explained.
  • Policing, Race & Crime: What the Data ShowsFBI crime data, use-of-force studies, and racial disparities in stops and shootings, with the reform and the law-and-order positions both tested against primary evidence.
  • Are Trans People Overrepresented as Mass Shooters?Politicians and political organizations have used this claim to justify legislation targeting the trans community. This article tests it against three databases, runs a sensitivity analysis, and checks the strongest opposing analysis against its own data.
  • Political, Ideological & Hate-Motivated Violence in the United StatesFar-right, far-left, Islamist, and religious violence, alongside hate crimes by bias category, with each ideology measured by the same evidentiary standard and each finding given a confidence rating.
  • Immigration and Crime: What the Data Actually ShowsIncarceration rates, Texas arrest data, 150 years of Census records, and the European evidence, tested against primary sources.
  • Sexual Orientation, Gender Identity, and CrimePer-capita crime data for heterosexual men and women, gay men, lesbian women, trans women, and trans men, plus the UK prison infographic, the gay predator myth, and victimization data.
  • The Death Penalty: Deterrence, Cost & Racial DisparitiesWhether capital punishment deters murder, whether it costs less than life imprisonment, and whether sentencing shows racial disparities, using NRC, GAO, state audits, BJS, and peer-reviewed studies.
  • The National DebtDeficit spending by administration, by Congress, and by policy area, from CBO and Treasury data.
  • Job Creation ClaimsEach administration's jobs record against BLS data, with context for policy lags, inherited recessions, and the Blinder-Watson finding on luck.
